# Home Coffee Brewing Basics

A short course for someone who has been making coffee with whatever was on
the counter and now wants to know what they are doing. By the end, the
student can pick a brewing method that fits their morning, run the brew
through cleanly, and read a bag of beans without bluffing.

## Pitch

Coffee at home should taste better than the gas station and not take
twenty minutes to set up. This course teaches three habits that get you
there: pick a method that matches the time you have, run the brew with
clean intent, and buy beans with eyes open. Three lessons, total reading
time about thirty minutes.

## Specs

- Audience: home brewer, no prior knowledge needed
- Format: text lessons with diagrams, one knowledge check
- Reading level: grade 8
- Total time: 30 to 40 minutes

## Outcomes

By the end of the course, the student can:

- Apply a method-selection rule to choose between drip, pour-over, and
  espresso based on the time and gear they have.
- Demonstrate the standard pour-over brew sequence from grind through
  serve.
- Identify the four lifecycle states a bean lives through and the cue
  that says it has moved between them.
